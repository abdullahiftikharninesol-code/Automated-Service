# 5-Minute Product Demonstration Script

**Target Audience:** Small Business Owners, Practice Managers, Service Business Founders  
**Demonstration Time:** 5 Minutes  
**Core Message:** *"Every customer inquiry gets captured, qualified, and followed up."*

---

## Preparation (Before the Demo)

- Have the **Missed Lead Rescue Dashboard** open on your screen (`/leads`).
- Have your phone ready with WhatsApp open to message the business number.
- Ensure the demo business is set up with generic qualification questions:
  1. What do you need help with?
  2. What date would you prefer?
  3. What city or location are you in?

---

## Minute 1: The Problem (60 Seconds)

> **Say to the prospect:**  
> *"Most small businesses lose 30% to 50% of their prospective customers simply because when someone reaches out on WhatsApp, you are busy on a job, in a meeting, or with another client — and by the time you reply hours later, that customer has already contacted your competitor."*
> 
> *"Missed Lead Rescue solves this in seconds: every inquiry is instantly captured, automatically qualified to collect the exact details you need, and tracked with automatic follow-up reminders so no lead ever falls through the cracks."*
> 
> *"Let me show you live how it works."*

---

## Minute 2: Instant Capture & Automatic Clarification (60 Seconds)

> **Action:**  
> Pick up your phone. Show the screen or type live into WhatsApp.  
> Send an incomplete message to the business WhatsApp number:  
> **"Hi, I need help."**

> **Point to phone screen:**  
> *"Notice that I only said 'Hi, I need help.' Most chatbots would give a frustrating canned response or ask nothing helpful. Watch what happens here within 3 seconds..."*

> **Action:**  
> The phone receives a response from the business:  
> *"What do you need help with? What date would you prefer?"*

> **Say:**  
> *"The system immediately realized it was missing the key details you need to price or schedule the job, and asked the customer directly in natural language."*

---

## Minute 3: Multi-turn Qualification Completion (60 Seconds)

> **Action:**  
> Reply from your phone:  
> **"I need a consultation this Friday in Islamabad."**

> **Point to phone screen:**  
> Within 2 seconds, the customer receives the confirmation:  
> *"Thanks, I’ve got the details. Someone from the team will get back to you shortly."*

> **Say:**  
> *"The customer now feels completely taken care of. They got an immediate, professional, 24/7 response, and they have zero reason to go look for your competitor."*

---

## Minute 4: The Owner Dashboard & Reminder Protection (60 Seconds)

> **Action:**  
> Switch to your computer screen showing the **Missed Lead Rescue Dashboard**. Refresh the lead inbox.

> **Point to dashboard:**  
> *"Look at your dashboard now. Without you having lifted a finger:"*
> 
> 1. *"The customer was automatically identified and added to your contacts."*
> 2. *"The lead is created with status **NEW** and qualification **COMPLETE**."*
> 3. *"Look at the structured details right here in the summary card: Service requested is **Consultation**, preferred date is **Friday**, location is **Islamabad**."*
> 4. *"Notice this countdown reminder right here: The system knows this customer is waiting on **YOU** (the owner). If you get busy and don't reply within 30 minutes, it sends you an alert so the lead is never forgotten."*

---

## Minute 5: Owner Reply & Instant Customer Delivery (60 Seconds)

> **Action:**  
> In the dashboard conversation thread, type a personalized reply:  
> **"Hi! We'd be glad to help this Friday. Does 2:00 PM work for you?"**  
> Click **Send Message**.

> **Show phone screen immediately:**  
> The WhatsApp message pops up on the customer's phone in real-time.

> **Point out in the dashboard:**  
> *"Notice what just happened in the system:"*
> - *"The reply reached the customer's phone instantly via official WhatsApp."*
> - *"The delivery badge shows **DELIVERED** (and updates to **READ** when the customer opens it)."*
> - *"The conversation automatically transitioned from 'Waiting on Owner' to 'Waiting on Customer'."*
> - *"And the reminder was automatically cancelled because you took care of it."*

---

## Closing Question (15 Seconds)

> **Say to the business owner:**  
> *"Imagine having this running 24/7 for your business: every customer inquiry captured, qualified, and followed up, without you ever missing another job.*  
> *Can we set this up for your business this afternoon? It only takes 15 minutes."*
